Hello! I'm Lyon Huang. I spend most of my time on the parts of a product people never really notice — browse pages, search, menus, the flows you run multiple times a day without thinking about them. Honestly that unglamorous stuff is my favorite part, since getting it right is what actually makes something easy to use.

I've always liked making things for other people to enjoy, and I think it started with games. Simulation games are still one of my favorite genres — I love the idea of building my own city, zoo, or theme park. There's something immensely satisfying about perfecting the supply chain for a space colony, or figuring out the right planting layout on a farm for maximum yield. That itch for optimization carried from those games into my work, where I'm always trying to make whatever someone's using just a little better.

My work has ranged from streaming platforms to theme-park apps, tiny features to full platform convergence — and most of what I'm proud of came out of working closely with the teams around me. Lately I've been heads-down on cross-platform and design-system updates, plus getting sharper with AI prototyping.
